Daraz Seller Ratings Explained: What to Actually Check

A seller's storefront page has more signal in it than the star rating alone — Daraz breaks performance into a few separate metrics, and reading them together tells you a lot more than the headline number does.

⭐Positive feedback percentage

Aim for 90%+

This is the closest thing to an overall trust score. Treat 90%+ as a reasonable baseline for most purchases, and go read a handful of the negative reviews specifically when a seller sits below that — a one-off complaint about packaging is very different from repeated reports of wrong or missing items.

📊Total review count, not just the percentage

Context matters

A seller with five 5-star reviews and one with two thousand 96%-positive reviews are not equally trustworthy, even though the smaller store's percentage looks higher. A larger review base gives you more confidence that the rating reflects real, consistent performance rather than a handful of friendly early reviewers.

🚚On-time delivery score

Time-sensitive buys

This tracks how often a seller's orders actually arrive within the window Daraz quoted you at checkout. It's a seller-specific pattern, not a platform-wide average — weigh it heavily if you're ordering something for a deadline, like a gift or an event.

💬Chat response rate and speed

Free signal

Daraz shows how quickly and how often a seller responds to buyer messages. A fast, specific response to a pre-purchase question is a strong positive signal; a seller who's slow or ignores messages entirely is worth being cautious about, especially for anything you might need to resolve an issue with after delivery.

The short version

Don't stop at the star rating. Check the total review count alongside the percentage, look at the on-time delivery score if timing matters, and consider messaging the seller directly for anything above a casual, low-stakes purchase — their response tells you almost as much as their numbers do.

FAQ

What's a good positive feedback percentage on Daraz?

Look for at least 90% positive feedback as a baseline. Below that, read a sample of the negative reviews specifically — a single bad batch complaint is different from a pattern of wrong items or non-delivery.

Does a high star rating mean a seller is trustworthy?

It's a good starting signal but not the whole picture, especially for a store with very few total ratings — five 5-star reviews look identical to a mature store with thousands, but carry far less statistical confidence. Check the total review count alongside the percentage, not just the percentage alone.

What does the "on-time delivery" score actually measure?

It tracks how often that seller's orders arrive within the delivery window Daraz estimated at checkout. A low score means a real, repeated pattern of late shipments from that specific seller — worth weighing heavily if your purchase is time-sensitive.

Is it worth messaging a seller before buying from them?

Yes, especially for a higher-value purchase or a seller you're unsure about. Response speed and specificity in Daraz's in-app chat is itself a signal — a seller who answers a direct question quickly and precisely is generally more reliable than one who's slow or vague.
