How to Get the Best Deals on Daraz

Daraz runs discounts constantly, which makes it easy to assume any "% off" badge is automatically a good deal. A few habits separate an actual discount from a listing that just looks discounted.

Collect vouchers before you shop, not at checkout

Free, easy to miss

Daraz's voucher collection page lists platform-wide, store-specific, and free-shipping vouchers that update daily — but most need to be "collected" to your account before they'll apply at checkout. Get in the habit of checking it before you start browsing, not after you've already filled your cart.

Stack vouchers where the terms allow it

Bigger savings

In many cases you can combine a platform voucher, a store voucher, and a bank or card-specific voucher on a single order. Each has its own conditions (minimum spend, eligible categories, expiry), so check the fine print on each one rather than assuming they'll all apply automatically.

Know the recurring sale-day calendar

3.3 through 12.12

Daraz runs recurring "same-digit" sale campaigns — 3.3, 5.5, 7.7, 9.9, 11.11, and 12.12 — with 11.11 typically the largest of the year. Timing a non-urgent purchase around one of these dates is usually worth the wait if you're not in a rush.

Verify the discount is real before trusting the badge

Worth 30 seconds

Some listings raise their "original" price shortly before a sale so the discounted price looks bigger than it is. Cross-check against the price you remember from a normal week, a price-history browser extension, or the same product on another retailer — a "% off" badge alone isn't proof of an actual discount.

The short version

Collect vouchers before you shop, stack what the terms allow, time non-urgent purchases around the sale-day calendar, and spend the 30 seconds to confirm a "% off" badge reflects a real discount rather than an inflated starting price.

FAQ

Can I use more than one voucher on the same Daraz order?

Often yes — Daraz allows stacking a platform voucher, a store voucher, and a bank/card voucher on the same order in many cases, as long as each one's terms are met. Check each voucher's conditions at checkout, since eligibility varies by voucher and category.

What are Daraz's biggest sale days?

The same-digit campaigns (3.3, 5.5, 7.7, 9.9, 11.11, 12.12) are Daraz's recurring major sale events, with 11.11 typically the largest. Category-specific and brand sales also run throughout the year outside these dates, so it's worth checking current promotions rather than only waiting for the big ones.

Do sale-day prices always beat the regular price?

Not automatically — some listings raise their price shortly before a sale, then "discount" back to close to the original. Comparing against a price history or your own notes from a normal week is the only way to confirm a sale price is a real discount, not just a display trick.

Where do I find and collect Daraz vouchers?

Daraz has a dedicated voucher collection page (in-app and on the website) where platform, store, and free-shipping vouchers are listed daily. Collect the ones relevant to your order before adding items to your cart, since a voucher usually needs to be "collected" to your account before it can be applied at checkout.
