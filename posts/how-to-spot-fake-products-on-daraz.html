Daraz's catalog is enormous, and that scale cuts both ways — genuine deals exist alongside counterfeit and misrepresented listings. Most fake-product complaints trace back to a few checkable signals that take under a minute to look for before you buy.

Check DarazMall first

Highest confidence

DarazMall listings carry a certified-brand guarantee — look for the thin blue bar above the product name, which marks it as verified official stock rather than a third-party marketplace listing. For branded electronics, cosmetics, or anything where authenticity really matters, starting your search in the DarazMall tab removes most of the risk before you even start comparing sellers.

Apply the "too cheap" rule

Red flag check

Compare the listing price against what the same item sells for on the brand's own site or other major retailers. If a marketplace listing is more than about 50% cheaper than everywhere else, treat that as a warning sign rather than a lucky find — legitimate sellers rarely undercut the market by that much on genuinely branded stock.

Read the listing like a skeptic

2-minute check

Poor grammar in the title or description, stock photos that don't match across multiple angles, zero or suspiciously generic reviews, and a seller with no other products in the same category are all signals worth pausing on. A legitimate seller's listing usually has specific, product-accurate photos and a reasonable spread of real customer reviews.

Message the seller before buying

Free and fast

Daraz's in-app chat lets you ask a seller directly about authenticity, warranty, or box condition before you commit. A seller who responds quickly and specifically is a good sign; one who's slow, vague, or pushes you to pay outside the app is a signal to walk away — Daraz's buyer protection only covers payments made through the platform.

The short version

For anything where authenticity actually matters — electronics, skincare, designer-labeled goods — start in DarazMall. For marketplace listings, run the price-comparison check, read the listing critically, and message the seller if anything feels off. None of this takes long, and it catches the vast majority of fake-product situations before you pay.

FAQ

What is DarazMall and how is it different from regular Daraz listings?

DarazMall is Daraz's certified-brand storefront — products listed there are verified as genuine, official-brand stock. Regular marketplace listings are sold by independent third-party sellers, who range from completely legitimate to selling counterfeits, so they need more scrutiny than a DarazMall listing does.

Does a cheap price always mean a product is fake?

Not always — genuine sales and clearance stock exist. But if a price is more than roughly 50% below what the same item sells for elsewhere, treat that as a strong warning sign rather than a lucky find, especially for branded electronics, cosmetics, and designer-labeled items.

Can I return a product if it turns out to be fake?

Yes — Daraz's return policy covers this. Marketplace-seller items generally have a 7-day return window and DarazMall items a 14-day window, but confirm the exact window on the listing itself since it can vary by product category.

What should I do if I already received a fake or counterfeit product?

Start a return through the Daraz app within the applicable return window, select the reason that matches (item not as described / counterfeit), and upload photos. Keep the original packaging until the return is resolved, since Daraz may request it back.
